Skooly Smart AI

An AI-powered smart school platform ensuring student safety through intelligent technology.

🏆 Best Innovative Product — Fikra 2026, INJAZ Saudi Arabia

Smarter Schools. Safer Students.

Skooly Smart AI is an integrated smart school operating platform specializing in managing student safety in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. It helps schools monitor students in real-time, manage attendance and dismissal, track school buses, and enhance instant communication with parents — all through a single intelligent system combining AI and IoT.
